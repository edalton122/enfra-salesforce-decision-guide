How we compare

Where ENFRA's needs point to Salesforce

Microsoft Dynamics 365 and HubSpot are both capable platforms. On the three challenges that matter most to ENFRA, here's how each one approaches them, based on each vendor's own public documentation.

The 18-month pursuit

Your criteria: “Milestones, projected vs. actual dates, and key activities” and “Exception reporting and management visibility.”
Salesforce

ENFRA's stages, projected and actual milestone dates, and exception flags live on the opportunity, with pipeline, slippage and exception dashboards built in. It's what you saw on October 19.8

HubSpot

Deal stages are standard. Tracking milestones as their own records requires custom objects, which are available only on Enterprise subscriptions.1

Microsoft Dynamics 365

Reporting and dashboards are included. For advanced analytics, Microsoft points to Power BI, a separate product.6

Snowflake, without a data project

Your criteria: “Integration approach, particularly with Snowflake” and “Data migration requirements.”
Salesforce

Data 360 Zero Copy with Snowflake works in both directions and is generally available. Your data stays where it lives, with no ETL pipelines to build or maintain.7

HubSpot

Snowflake integrations require Data Hub Enterprise and sync on a schedule you choose, from hourly to daily.2

Microsoft Dynamics 365

Dynamics 365 data reaches Snowflake through Microsoft Fabric (Link to Fabric and OneLake) or through Snowflake's Dataverse connector, which replicates selected tables.4

Questions to ask every vendor

Put every platform to the same test

Use these with each vendor in your evaluation, including us. Check them off as you get answers.

  • 01

    Can we track projected vs. actual dates for every milestone and flag exceptions, on the edition you're quoting?

  • 02

    Which edition or add-on is required for custom objects and a sandbox to test changes?

  • 03

    Does our Snowflake data stay in Snowflake, or is it synced or copied? How often, and in which directions?

  • 04

    Which products are required for CRM, email campaigns, analytics and AI, and what does each include?

  • 05

    After go-live, can our own team change stages, fields and reports without a partner?

  • 06

    What does it take to extend from 25 EaaS users to other ENFRA teams?
